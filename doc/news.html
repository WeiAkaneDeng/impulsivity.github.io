News & Updates

Recent publications, grants, conferences, and milestones from the Deng Lab and collaborators.

2025

GWAS of Delay Discounting in Children Published

Deng et al. publish the first genome-wide association study of impulsivity in children using data from the ABCD Study. The analysis identified novel loci associated with delay discounting across ages 9-10, with implications for early intervention. Published in Genes, Brain and Behavior.

Citation: Deng WQ, et al. (2025). Genome-wide association study of delay discounting in childhood. Genes, Brain and Behavior, in press.

2024

Longitudinal Impulsivity GWAS in Molecular Psychiatry

New paper reveals heritability of delay discounting and sensation seeking across development. Longitudinal analysis spanning 8+ years identifies temporal stability of genetic influences and gene-by-age interactions. Accepted in Molecular Psychiatry.

Key finding: Genetic effects on impulsivity strengthen during adolescence, with implications for developmental psychiatry.

2024

PheWAS of Alcohol-Related PGS Published

High-resolution phenome-wide association study reveals pleiotropic effects of polygenic risk scores for alcohol use disorder. Analysis of UK Biobank demonstrates associations with psychiatric comorbidities, metabolic traits, and behavioral phenotypes. Published in Alcohol and Alcoholism.

2023

Polygenic Prediction Paper in Genes, Brain and Behavior

Systematic evaluation of 11 polygenic risk scores for impulsivity prediction across independent cohorts. Study assesses predictive accuracy in European ancestry samples and examines cross-ethnic transferability. Provides benchmark comparisons and recommendations for PGS selection in research and clinical applications.

2022

gJLS2 R Package Released on CRAN

gJLS2 (Joint Location and Scale) statistical genetics package now available on CRAN and GitHub. Novel method for X-chromosome inclusive GWAS with applications to sex-specific genetic architecture of complex traits. Includes vignettes, documentation, and example data.

Repository: weiakanedeng/gJLS2
