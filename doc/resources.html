Open Science

Resources & Tools

A curated collection of assessment instruments, software packages, datasets, and educational materials to support research in self-regulation and delay discounting.

Behavioral Assessment

Assessment Tools

Validated instruments and questionnaires for measuring impulsivity, delay discounting, and addiction-related phenotypes.

Behavioral Economics & Delay Discounting

📊

Monetary Choice Questionnaire (MCQ-27)

Kirby et al. (1999) – 27-item measure of delay discounting with real monetary choices. Standard assessment in behavioral economics research.

💰

Alcohol Purchase Task (APT)

Behavioral economic assessment of alcohol demand curve, price sensitivity, and motivation. Used in addiction and self-control studies.

Impulsivity & Self-Control

🎯

UPPS-P Impulsive Behavior Scale

Lynam et al. (2007) – 59-item five-factor measure assessing negative urgency, lack of premeditation, lack of perseverance, sensation seeking, and positive urgency.

🔍

AUDIT (Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test)

WHO-developed 10-item screening tool for hazardous and harmful alcohol use. Widely used in population studies and clinical settings.

Computational Tools

Software & Packages

Open-source software for statistical genetics, genome-wide association studies, and polygenic risk score analysis.

GWAS & Statistical Genetics

📦

gJLS2 (R package)

Deng et al. – Joint location and scale (JLS) analysis for X-inclusive genome-wide association studies. Available on CRAN and GitHub: weiakanedeng/gJLS2

🔬

PLINK

Whole genome association analysis toolset. Enables filtering, association analysis, and population-stratification detection. Industry standard for GWAS.

🎲

PRSice-2

High-resolution polygenic risk score software. Enables flexible PRS computation, clumping, and threshold selection for complex trait prediction.

📈

LDSC (LD Score Regression)

Heritability estimation and genetic correlation analysis using GWAS summary statistics. Corrects for confounding and population structure.

Data Resources

Datasets & Cohorts

Large-scale population and clinical cohorts with genomic data, behavioral phenotypes, and longitudinal assessments.

Population Cohorts & Biobanks

👶

ABCD Study

Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development – N ≈ 12,000 children (ages 9-10+). Longitudinal assessment of brain development, cognition, behavior, and genetics. Learn more

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

ALSPAC

Avon Longitudinal Study of Parents and Children – UK birth cohort with continuous assessment from pregnancy through adulthood. Rich behavioral and genetic data.

🏥

UK Biobank

Large population biobank (N > 500,000) with detailed health, behavioral, and genomic data. Extensive phenotype coverage and open access through approved applications.

🗂️

PGS Catalog

Polygenic Score (PGS) Catalog – Comprehensive repository of published polygenic scores with validation metrics, effect sizes, and cross-ethnic applicability data.

Educational Resources

Foundational Topics

📚

Delay Discounting: Theory & Measurement

Overview of temporal discounting, hyperbolic vs exponential models, and relationship to self-control, addiction, and decision-making under uncertainty.

🧬

GWAS Primer for Behavioral Scientists

Introduction to genome-wide association studies, effect sizes, Manhattan plots, interpreting significance thresholds, and understanding heritability estimates.

🔬

Epigenetics Fundamentals

DNA methylation, histone modifications, and environmental interactions. How epigenetic changes relate to behavioral and psychiatric phenotypes across the lifespan.

Key Review Papers

Curated collection of landmark publications in delay discounting, impulsivity genetics, and translational addiction research. Accessible to researchers at all career stages.
