Key Pathway Connections

📊

Genetic Basis

Genome-wide association studies (GWAS) identify genetic variants (CADM2, NCAM1, DRD2) that influence impulsivity and delay discounting traits.

🧬

Epigenetic Regulation

DNA methylation patterns (EWAS, DNAm-age) mediate genetic effects and environmental influences on self-regulation capacity.

🧠

Neural Mechanisms

Brain regions (vmPFC, striatum, lPFC) involved in reward processing, impulse control, and decision-making are shaped by genetic and epigenetic factors.

⚡

Behavioral Phenotypes

Measurable traits like delay discounting, negative urgency, and sensation-seeking emerge from integrated neural and genetic influences.

❤️

Health Consequences

Individual differences in self-regulation predict vulnerability to obesity, cardiovascular disease, addiction, and psychiatric disorders.

🔬

Assessment Methods

Standardized measures (MCQ, UPPS-P, gJLS2) quantify behavioral phenotypes for research and clinical applications.
