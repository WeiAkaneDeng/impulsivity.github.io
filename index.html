Wei Q. Deng, PhD

Assistant Professor
Department of Psychiatry and Behavioural Neurosciences
McMaster University
Peter Boris Centre for Addictions Research

Wei Q. Deng's research spans statistical genetics, genome-wide association studies (GWAS), polygenic risk scores, epigenetics, and the developmental trajectories of delay discounting across the lifespan. Her work integrates computational genomics, methylation profiling, and behavioral economics to understand individual differences in self-regulation.

Featured Collaborator

Centre Director and key collaborator in understanding the behavioral economics and neurobiology of self-regulation

James MacKillop, PhD

Peter Boris Chair in Addiction Research
Director, Peter Boris Centre for Addictions Research
McMaster University
Department of Psychiatry and Behavioural Neurosciences

Dr. MacKillop's research focuses on behavioral economics, neuroeconomics, delay discounting, and addictive behaviors. His meta-analyses and theoretical work have shaped the field's understanding of how temporal discounting relates to addiction and other health outcomes.
